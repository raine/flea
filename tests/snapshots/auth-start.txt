Sign in to Tori

1. Open this URL:

https://login.vend.fi/oauth/authorize?client_id=client&state=oauth-state

2. Finish signing in.
3. When the browser asks, choose Open ToriAuthHelper.app. The Vend tab may keep showing ‘Kirjaudutaan’; you can close it after the helper opens.
4. Return here and run:

tori auth complete flow-1

Complete these steps within 10 minutes.
